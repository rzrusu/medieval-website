What is Medieval Roleplay?

Medieval Roleplay is an upcoming server for the GTA: San Andreas Multiplayer modification. Enter the feudal world of the 13th century Holy Roman Empire. Make your destiny and forge a worthy name on one of SA:MP's most ambitious projects ever.

« Features »

IMMERSIVE EXPERIENCE

Live and die in a true recreation of medieval times. Fight, trade, build, conquer in this mysterious and intriguing realm of feudal politics, lords, and greed.

CHARACTERS

Develop your characters through hundreds of new opportunities, skills, traits, and flaws in a dedicated community. Live your own story in a land of knights, deception, and religious fever.

NEW MAP

Experience 13th century Holy Roman Empire at its core. Cross lengthy rivers, camp in lush forests, visit taverns and have a drink or simply explore the lands sprawling with villages and castles.

COMBAT

Fight your foe through an innovative, intuitive combat system. Fiercely handle longswords, shields, axes bows, and more while mastering and honing your skills and abilities.

TRADING

Trade your way around the lands. Go on expeditions and discover new business ventures. Invest goods and resources for profit. Build your enterprise in a feudal world.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • When is the server launching?
    • There is no set date for the launch. The base systems are already done, right now we're focusing on the map to launch as soon as possible. The map will be a custom model, so naturally, it will take a bit longer than usual.
  • Are you holding closed tests?
    • We sometimes hold closed betas for our verified members to partake in and help find bugs. If you would like to participate you can join the discord, be active and helpful and a staff member may give you the Verified role.
  • Where does the server take place?
    • The server takes place in the 13th century Holy Roman Empire. It features a variety of villages, towns, castles, farms, and more. In the future, after release, we plan on extending the map to further include more of Central Europe.
  • Do I need a good PC?
    • We've made our server as accessible as possible to lower-end machines. Our recent strides in map optimization have led us to be able to render massive maps without any major performance hits. If you can run SA:MP, you're unlikely to face any problems here.
  • Where can I see more?
    • Join our Discord where we post daily & weekly updates on our progress as well as many of our announcements. You can also chat with other members and help us get farther through the word of mouth.
