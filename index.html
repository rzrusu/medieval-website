What is Medieval Roleplay?

Medieval Roleplay is an upcoming server for the GTA: San Andreas Multiplayer modification. Enter a medieval portrayal of the Holy Roman Empire in the 13th century. Make your own destiny and forge a worthy dynasty on one of SA:MP's most ambitious projects ever.

« Features »

IMMERSIVE EXPERIENCE

Live and interact in a true recreation of the medieval times. Become a priest, a knight, a merchant or even a simple peasant. Dedicated community and staff team ensure a fun and fair environment.

CHARACTERS

Develop your character through hundreds of opportunities, skills, traits, flaws in a dedicated community of players. Create connections, build networks and grow your character with community support.

FEUDAL WORLD

Live, die or immortalise yourself in a world of deception, feudal politics and religious fever. Shape your future and destiny however you want in a realistic medieval world.

TRADING

Trade all around the Holy Roman Empire in the 13th century. Go on expeditions and voyages to discover new mines, goods and resources. Refurbish items and sell them at a profit.

COMBAT

Battle your opponents through a scripted combat system. Based on Mount&Blade, the system allows you to fiercely handle a sword, axe, bow and more. Make yourself worthy with a sheer cut.

BUILT FOR EVERYONE

Even on low-end PCs, enjoying the spoils and mysteries of a medieval world are possible. You will even be able to customize a wide range of settings to better your performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • When is the server launching?
    • There is no set date on launch. The base systems are already done, right now we're polishing the existing systems whilst we wait for the map to be finished. The map will be a custom model, so naturally it will take a bit longer than usual.
  • Are you holding closed tests?
    • We sometimes hold closed betas for our verified members to partake in and help find bugs. If you would like to participate you can join the discord, be active and helpful and a staff member may give you the Verified role.
  • Where does the server take place?
    • The server takes place in 13th century Holy Roman Empire. It features over 10+ different villages, towns, farms and more. In the future, after release, we plan on extending the map to further include more of Central Europe.
  • When is the server launching?
    • There is no set date on launch. The base systems are already done, right now we're polishing the existing systems whilst we wait for the map to be finished. The map will be a custom model, so naturally it will take a bit longer than usual.
  • Do I need a good PC?
    • No, not really. We have made great strides to our rendering engine, now being able to render a large map without any major performance hits on lower end machines. Even if you are slightly affected, you will be able to set your own draw distance or even disable foliage and more in the settings.
  • Where can I see more?
    • Join our Discord where we post daily & weekly updates on our progress as well as many of our announcements. You can also chat with other members and help us get farther through the word of mouth.
