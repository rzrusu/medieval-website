What is Medieval Roleplay?

Medieval Roleplay is an upcoming server for the GTA: San Andreas Multiplayer modification. Play as a medieval knight, priest or even a poor peasant in a feudal world of war, intrigue and politics. Travel a completely custom modelled map built from scratch for SA:MP's most ambitious project ever.

IMMERSIVE EXPERIENCE

Experience the medieval ages through one of the most immersive roleplay experiences in the entire SA:MP community. Develop your character through hundreds of opportunities, skills, traits, flaws in a dedicated community of players.
